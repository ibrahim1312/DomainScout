    How the estimate is calculated

    DomainScout starts with a USD baseline for the extension and adjusts it for lexical quality. This is a heuristic for a retail asking-price scenario. An actual sale can occur outside the displayed range, and a domain may never sell.

    Each comparable needs a source, the same suffix after the first label, and a first label within five characters of the target. Repeated names are excluded. The sales median contributes 17% per sale, up to 85%. Smaller samples use wider price ranges. The site does not verify sources, check trademarks, measure demand, or retrieve live sales.

    Estimated profit subtracts acquisition cost, renewals, and commission from the midpoint. Cost assumptions apply to the current scenario across domains. Domain lists and inputs are processed in your browser.
