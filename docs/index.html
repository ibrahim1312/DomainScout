    How the estimate is calculated

    DomainScout starts with conservative USD baselines for extensions. It adjusts for length, domain type, hyphens and digits. The quick-sale scenario is 20% of the retail midpoint. Both are heuristics. An actual sale can fall outside the range, and a domain may never sell.

    Comparables need a source, the same suffix, similar length, and a similar use of digits or hyphens. Repeated names are excluded. The model blends the sales median and baseline using a capped price ratio and a weight of 12% per sale, up to 68%. Smaller samples use wider ranges. The site does not verify sources, check trademarks, measure demand, or retrieve live sales.

    Estimated profit subtracts acquisition cost, renewals, and commission from the midpoint. Cost assumptions apply to the current scenario across domains. Valuation inputs stay in your browser. Buyer lookup sends generated variants to Google Public DNS.
